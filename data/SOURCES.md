# Data sources for GrantScape

All inputs are public-domain U.S. government data. GrantScape ships **no real
NIH data** — only clearly-labeled *synthetic* example files (below). Download
the official tables and load them in the app.

## 1. Funding Map — RCDC categorical spending
- Source: NIH RePORT, "Estimates of Funding for Various Research, Condition,
  and Disease Categories (RCDC)"
- URL: https://report.nih.gov/funding/categorical-spending
- Download the table as CSV (FY2008–present). Load in the **Funding Map** tab.
- Expected shape: one column of category names + one column per fiscal year of
  dollar amounts. (Mortality/prevalence columns are ignored automatically.)

## 2. Concept Landscape — institutes & study sections
- Generated by `scripts/pull_concept_landscape.py` (queries NIH RePORTER).
- Set your keywords/period/mechanism in the script CONFIG, run it, then load
  `concept_landscape_by_ic.csv` and `concept_landscape_by_studysection.csv`.
- RePORTER contains **funded projects only** — these are "where funded work in
  the area lands," not success rates.

## 3. Institute Odds — success rates by IC
- Source: NIH RePORT "Success Rates" (or NIH Data Book).
- URL: https://report.nih.gov/nihdatabook/  (Success Rates section)
- Load a tidy CSV with at least an institute column and a success-rate column;
  optional activity-code / fiscal-year columns enable filtering.
- These rates are **administrative and IC-level**, not specific to your topic.

## Example (SYNTHETIC) files in this folder
