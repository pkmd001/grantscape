GrantScape — the NIH funding landscape for grant strategy

Three views of where NIH funds, where the money is moving, and where your science is reviewed — built from public NIH data. Nothing is uploaded; all computation runs in your browser.

What NIH funds by research category, and which areas are rising. Load the official RCDC categorical-spending table (CSV) from report.nih.gov.






“Relative to portfolio” subtracts the median category growth, isolating fields rising faster than NIH overall (removing the macro-budget trend). Click a category for its funding trajectory.

GrantScape • public-domain NIH data (report.nih.gov, RePORTER, NIH Data Book). Not affiliated with or endorsed by NIH. RCDC categories overlap and NIH does not budget by category; success rates by IC are administrative and not topic-specific; study-section success rates are not published.
